VERDICT: CHANGES REQUIRED

Locations below refer to `docs/decisions/0003-code-reference.md`.

1. **C6, replay 3, slice test 3 — redaction misses decoded strings.**  
   **Problem:** Checking only raw TOML cannot enforce the claimed rejection of identifying text. A VIN encoded using TOML Unicode escapes passes the raw scan and becomes a VIN after parsing.  
   **Evidence:** [TOML 1.0 permits Unicode escapes in strings](https://toml.io/en/v1.0.0#string). This bypass is separate from G8’s stated residual for separated or truncated VINs.  
   **Smallest fix:** Retain the raw scan for comments; also scan every decoded string. Add an escaped-token rejection case.

2. **C6, C9 contribution statement — permission to read is treated as permission to publish.**  
   **Problem:** Someone allowed to diagnose another person’s car can publish its fault history, date, platform and contributor association. The statement attests permission to read, not permission to disclose. Private export inspection also has no handling boundary.  
   **Evidence:** C9 line 71 requires only ownership or permission to read; Q4 acknowledges vehicle-history disclosure only for the maintainer’s seed entries. DCO clause (d) acknowledges publication of the contribution; it does not establish the vehicle owner’s consent.  
   **Smallest fix:** Require an explicit attestation that publishing the submitted observation metadata is authorized. State that exports are not requested publicly, require a separate voluntary private disclosure, and define retention/deletion expectations. Record that hashes and handles can link observations without identifying the car directly.

3. **C5, Q3 — “independent sources” has no operational acceptance rule.**  
   **Problem:** Two handles and two export hashes establish neither independent people nor independent sources of meaning. Two genuine readings using the same tool text satisfy the loader while failing C5’s definition.  
   **Evidence:** C5 calls its check a floor, but supplies no reviewer criterion or attestation for independence. Q3 acknowledges the common-source problem without specifying how either answer changes acceptance of `corroborated`.  
   **Smallest fix:** Leave Q3 open, but explicitly block accepting `corroborated` claims until its answer yields a reviewable independence rule and contribution attestation. Describe the loader as checking stamp distinctness only.

4. **C9 line 73, replay 8 — D6 is overstated as an injection boundary.**  
   **Problem:** Vehicle-read-only operation does not make the AI client read-only. Accepted DR-0002 G6 permits garage additions, supersessions and retractions. A hostile description can therefore target those writes, other client tools or disclosures.  
   **Evidence:** C9 cites “v0 being read-only (D6)” as bounding the risk; D6 concerns vehicle state. C8 specifies rendering but no explicit boundary identifying reference text as untrusted data.  
   **Smallest fix:** Say “vehicle-read-only”; name garage writes and other client capabilities as residual exposure. Require reference text to remain explicitly identified as untrusted reference data in outgoing results, separate from instructions and measured provenance. Assign that check to the CLI/MCP integration slice. Do not claim this prevents client-side injection.

5. **Q1 lines 181–182, authority row 157 — licence summaries overstate the sources and settle an unanswered legal question.**  
   **Problem:** “Every copy” requiring a change notice is inaccurate; the deed requires indicating **whether** changes were made and permits reasonable attribution methods. “Changing later means asking every past contributor or dropping their entries” is an unsupported categorical conclusion. “CC0: nothing … to carry” also needs its limited scope stated.  
   **Evidence:** The [CC BY deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) includes exceptions and qualifications. [CC0 §4](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.txt) excludes patent/trademark rights and disclaims clearing other persons’ rights. Neither source establishes the record’s universal relicensing conclusion.  
   **Smallest fix:** Report the sources’ qualified terms; distinguish licence-text summaries from applicability. Replace the relicensing sentence with a risk that a different licence may require additional permissions and cannot revoke existing grants. Keep Q1 open.

6. **C10 line 80 — D8 is misquoted.**  
   **Problem:** “A local tool that imports the user’s own SGBD texts (D8 prohibits it)” states a broader prohibition than the accepted record.  
   **Evidence:** D8 expressly permits a local tool to read the user’s copies; it prohibits proprietary tables entering the repository.  
   **Smallest fix:** Specify “imports … into the published reference.” Do not prohibit local reading under D8.

7. **C6 line 49 versus line 52 and authority row 152 — privacy promise exceeds its definition.**  
   **Problem:** “Never holds … an IP address” conflicts with a check covering only IPv4 dotted quads. MAC shape and dotted-quad matching are called “our own definitions,” but those definitions are absent.  
   **Evidence:** IPv6 is outside the stated check; “MAC-address-shaped” leaves separators, compact forms and token boundaries unspecified.  
   **Smallest fix:** Define the chosen matching shapes once, including boundaries, and explicitly state unsupported forms as residuals. Distinguish prohibited content from mechanically detected content.

8. **C7, Q2 line 184, slice lines 169–177 — storage and slice contracts disagree.**  
   **Problem:** Q2 quotes C7 as saying “kept in this repo,” although C7 now defers location to Q2. The slice promises parsing from a string with no file I/O, but requires matching a header against an unspecified path input. Q2’s separate-repository outcome is treated as a stop condition without distinguishing loader work from the repository gate.  
   **Evidence:** Lines 56, 169, 171, 175, 177 and 184.  
   **Smallest fix:** Remove the stale quotation; specify caller-supplied expected platform/address or move path validation to the repository gate. Mark only location-dependent gate work as awaiting Q2.
